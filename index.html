      Click the viewport and the mouse is captured: it disappears, raw movement turns the view, and Esc hands it back (and pauses). That is the default and it works exactly like a native FPS. If the page is embedded somewhere that refuses pointer lock, the game falls back to free look — the mouse moves your reticle and pushing it past the two tick marks swings the view — and every click asks for capture again. Open the HTML file directly for guaranteed mouse capture. Change the mode under Look mode in Options; , and . turn from the keyboard. Backspace resets stuck input; F3 shows a live input readout and F2 the raw mouse stream.

      Lost? The corner radar (N) marks objectives, and the watch (Tab) shows the whole floor plan with every outstanding objective lettered on it and its range in metres.

      Aim mode narrows your spread and zooms in — the only way the KB-Klobb hits anything past four metres.

      Keep moving. Hostile aim tracks a sprinting agent badly — worst on AGENT clearance — and crouching makes you harder to hit again. Standing still is how you get shot.
