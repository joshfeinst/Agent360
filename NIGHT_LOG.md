# Night log — the v1.23 player-session round

Ten personas were handed the build cold and told to play it — first-timer,
returning player, phone-in-landscape, keyboard-only, colour-blind, reduced-
motion, completionist, cheater, speedrunner, lapsed-and-back. Every anomaly
they filed went to a paired skeptic whose job was to refute it: drive it again
with real input, on a real route, and throw it out if the harness rather than
the game produced it. **39 reported, 29 confirmed, 10 refuted** — the refuted
ten were harness artifacts (25 keypresses/second, `setInputFiles` where a
player uses a dialog), measurements of the wrong thing, or design the game
states in its own words. Twelve items of pure friction were adjudicated
alongside them. It landed as two commits: the half a phone player meets, then
the half everybody else does.

| Round | What changed | Why the numbers said so | How it was verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · The phone gets its hands back** | Pause and briefing action rows sticky to their own scrollport and every screen opening at its top; menus activate their own buttons on `touchend`; one owner per look drag with a separate tap slot; tap-to-fire clocked on event timestamps (600ms); drag-look's per-frame budget scaled to the frame actually drawn, surplus carried; two-finger regrip inherits a held button; `clearInput(keepHeld)` so an in-window pause keeps the keyboard, the FIRE hold and the AIM/CROUCH latches; free-look decay scaled by sensitivity; right-pad pitch 46→24 px/s with a vertical dead zone; USE answers a shootable objective; the interact ring starts on contact and states its 1.9s; contact beats bearing on a hack; HUD clock/KEY/OBJ on the same dark plate the bars use; waypoint points at the NEAREST outstanding objective; pickup line above its objective line; M01's UPS-cage jamb un-wedged; an OPTIONS row on the watch | The watch had **zero tappable controls** on a landscape phone: `fit()` snaps `#frame` to 640×360, the panel is 522px at 844×390 and 857px at 667×375, its action row is the last child (y 469 / y 797), and `show()` hides the touch cluster in the same instant.
